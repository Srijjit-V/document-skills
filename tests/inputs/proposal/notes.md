Eastside Neighbors Association (applicant). Contact: Rosa Delgado, program lead.
Grant: City Neighborhood Grant. Deadline December 1, 2026. We are asking for $18,500.

Idea: Mobile Tool Library. A used cargo van stocked with basic tools (drills, ladders, saws, garden tools) that visits three neighborhoods each week and lends tools for free.

Why: We surveyed 212 households in September 2026. 38% said they lack basic tools for home repairs. People told us buying tools for one repair is not worth it, and repairs get put off.

Plan:
- Buy a used cargo van and shelving (January to February 2027)
- Stock starter tool inventory (February to March 2027)
- Train 20 volunteers (March 2027)
- Launch Saturday April 3, 2027
- Van visits three neighborhoods weekly: Tuesday evenings and Saturday mornings
- Tools are logged out with a one-page waiver, 7 day loans

Goals for the first year (to April 2028): 400 tool loans, 150 different households served.

Risks: van breakdown (we budget a maintenance fund from donations, not from this grant), tools not returned (waiver, sign-out log, and a small deposit for expensive items), volunteer turnover (20 trained, plan to train 10 more in September 2027).
We will report quarterly to the committee.
