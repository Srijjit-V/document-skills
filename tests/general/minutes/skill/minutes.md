# Harbor Street Community Garden Committee: Minutes

**Meeting:** Tuesday, October 21, Library room B. Called to order 7:04 p.m., adjourned 7:31 p.m.
**Present:** Maria (chair), Tom, Priya, Luis, Ann
**Apologies:** Devon

## Decisions

1. **Raised beds approved, 4 to 1.** The committee will spend $2,400 on eight raised beds from Harbor Lumber ($2,310 lumber plus $90 screws and corner brackets). Luis moved and Ann seconded. In favor: Maria, Luis, Ann, Priya. Opposed: Tom, who preferred to wait for lower spring lumber prices and asked that his objection be on record.
2. **Shed postponed to spring.** No objection. A 6 by 8 kit costs about $1,100, and the budget cannot cover the shed and the beds. It returns at the March meeting.

## Discussion

- **Treasurer's report (Ann):** The balance was $5,180 as of October 20, after the seed order. Nothing else came in.
- **Raised beds:** Priya argued for building in December while volunteers are available. Tom argued for waiting for spring pricing.
- **Build day:** Priya proposed Saturday, December 6, 9 a.m. It needs 12 volunteers and 7 have said yes. The date was proposed, not voted on.
- **Water:** The hookup needs a permit with an $85 fee. The person Tom spoke to was not the right contact. Priya asked whether a hookup is allowed on that side of the fence. Tom does not know yet.
- **Plots:** Plots are now limited to residents of Harbor Street and the two blocks around it. Some people asked about opening a few to outsiders. Ann has no proposal yet. Tom wants it to address the waitlist, since residents have waited two years.
- **Parking lot repaving:** Ann raised a rumor that could affect build day. Tom said the city has not announced anything. It was not taken up as an item.
