# Chapter 7 water cycle and watersheds: exam review
Source: Ms. Reyes's Ch. 7 notes; exam Fri Nov 6, 40 questions
> **If you only read one thing:** the water cycle has no starting point, only about 2.5% of Earth's water is fresh, and a watershed is the land that drains to one outlet.

## Freshwater numbers ★
| Share | Of what |
|---|---|
| 97% | salt water (oceans) |
| 2.5% | fresh water |
| 69% | of fresh: glaciers, ice caps |
| 30% | of fresh: groundwater |
| under 1% | of fresh: lakes, rivers, swamps |

Only a tiny share of fresh water is easy for people to use.

## Cycle terms ★
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Evaporation | liquid to vapor, solar heat, any temperature |
| Transpiration | plants release vapor via leaves |
| Condensation | vapor cools to droplets: clouds |
| Precipitation | rain, snow, sleet, hail |
| Runoff | flows over land to streams |
| Infiltration | soaks into soil; can recharge groundwater |

## Order of the cycle ★
1. Evaporation
2. Transpiration
3. Condensation
4. Precipitation
5. Runoff
6. Infiltration

Many paths; not one fixed line.

## Residence time
| Place | About |
|---|---|
| Atmosphere | 9 days |
| Rivers | 2 weeks |
| Ocean | 3,200 years |
| Glaciers | thousands of years |

## Groundwater words
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Aquifer | rock or sediment holding and moving groundwater |
| Water table | top of fully saturated zone; rises and falls |
| Recharge | infiltration adds water to aquifer |

## Watersheds ★
- Land that drains to one outlet (river, lake, sea).
- Boundary: a ridge or high ground, the divide.
- Fertilizer, pollution, paving all reach the outlet.
- Pavement → less infiltration → more runoff → faster stream rise after storms.

`Runoff = Precip - Infiltration - Evaporation`

Ignores plants and storage.

Example (practice numbers): 100 - 30 - 50 = 20 runoff.

## Ms. Reyes says know ★
- The terms.
- The order of the cycle.
- What a watershed is.
- The freshwater numbers.

## Common mistakes ⚠
- ⚠ Transpiration is plants; evaporation is surfaces, because transpiration comes from living leaves.
- ⚠ The water table is not flat, because it follows the land a little.
- ⚠ The cycle does not start at the ocean, because it has no starting point.
- ⚠ Fresh water is about 2.5%, not "most" water, because oceans hold about 97%.
