Ch. 7 Cram Sheet: Water Cycle and Watersheds

Exam Fri Nov 6, 40 questions. Know: terms, cycle order, watershed, freshwater numbers.

Freshwater numbers

  • Salt water (oceans): ~97%. Fresh water: ~2.5% (not "most").
  • Of the fresh: ~69% glaciers/ice caps, ~30% groundwater, <1% surface water.

Cycle order

No starting point; many paths.

  1. Evaporation: liquid to vapor, sun's heat, any temperature.
  2. Transpiration: plants release vapor from leaves.
  3. Condensation: vapor cools to droplets; makes clouds.
  4. Precipitation: rain, snow, sleet, hail.
  5. Runoff: flows over land to streams and rivers.
  6. Infiltration: soaks into soil; can recharge groundwater.

Residence time

How long a molecule stays in one place.

Atmosphere~9 days
Rivers~2 weeks
Ocean~3,200 years
Icethousands of years

Groundwater

  • Aquifer: rock or sediment layer that holds and moves groundwater.
  • Water table: top of the fully saturated zone. Rises and falls. NOT flat.
  • Recharge: water added to an aquifer by infiltration.

Watersheds

  • Land area draining to one outlet (river, lake, sea).
  • Edge = divide (ridge or high ground).
  • Fertilizer, pollution, paving upstream affect the outlet.
  • Paving: less infiltration, more runoff, streams rise faster after storms.

Runoff = Precipitation − Infiltration − Evaporation

(Ignores plants and storage.)

Ms. Reyes's warnings

  • Transpiration = plants. Evaporation = surfaces.
  • Water table is not flat.
  • The cycle does not start at the ocean.
  • Fresh water is ~2.5%, not "most."
