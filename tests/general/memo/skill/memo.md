Please approve a 60-day pilot of NorthRoute for domestic lower-48 parcels, starting January 6, 2027, and tell me by November 15. NorthRoute quoted $6.95 per parcel against SwiftShip's $8.40, which saves about $1,667 a month at our 1,150 parcels. The cost is an earlier packing cutoff and a damage rate we have not verified.

## Why

The saving is ($8.40 - $6.95) x 1,150 = $1,667 a month, computed from the quote and our current volume. Alaska and Hawaii are 2.1% of orders and NorthRoute does not serve them, so they stay on SwiftShip and the real saving is slightly lower.
